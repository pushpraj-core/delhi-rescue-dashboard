# Model Card: Raksha Mumbai Triage & Hotspot Models

## Model Details
- **Developer**: Raksha Mumbai Team
- **Model Versions**: 1.0.0
- **Model Types**: 
  - Triage: LightGBM Multiclass Classifier
  - Hotspot: LightGBM Regressor
- **Architecture**: Gradient Boosting Decision Trees (GBDT)

## Intended Use
- **Primary Use Cases**:
  - **Triage**: Automatically route and prioritize incoming citizen reports based on urgency (1-Critical to 5-Low).
  - **Hotspot**: Forecast the number of expected incidents in a given H3 resolution-8 geographic cell for the next day.
- **Out-of-Scope**: Not to be used as the sole decision-maker for emergency response dispatch without human-in-the-loop validation by a Nodal Officer.

## Factors
- **Demographic/Geographic**: Trained strictly on synthetic data bounded to Mumbai geographic limits.
- **Features Used**: Hour of day, day of week, month, lat/lng, ward, category, user confidence score.

## Metrics
- **Triage Model**: Target F1-Score across all classes > 0.85.
- **Hotspot Model**: Target Mean Absolute Error (MAE) < 2.0 incidents per cell per day.

## Training Data
- Synthetic data generated by `ml/data/generate.py`. Models simulate real-world child rescue scenarios (Abuse, Trafficking, Labour, etc.).

## Ethical Considerations
- The models process geographic and categorical data. No personally identifiable information (PII) is used as inputs. The models err on the side of caution (predicting higher urgency for certain categories inherently) to prevent under-prioritizing critical rescue cases.
